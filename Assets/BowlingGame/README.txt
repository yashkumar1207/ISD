BOWLING NIGHT — Unity primitive-built bowling game

Open the SampleScene and press Play. Use A/D to aim, W/S to adjust power, and Space or the on-screen ROLL button to roll. The ball uses a Rigidbody, pins use simple colliders and rigidbodies, and knocked pins are counted after each roll. Play 10 frames; the score panel tracks pins knocked down and shows strike/spare messages.

Built with Unity primitive shapes, C# variables, methods, conditions, loops, keyboard input, UI buttons, and basic physics. No Blender models or external assets are used.
